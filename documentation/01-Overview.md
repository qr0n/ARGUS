# ARGUS Documentation: Project Overview

This document serves as the official documentation for the **ARGUS** project. It provides a high-level overview of the project's purpose, aims, end users, target audience, and its overall impact.

---

## Purpose
The primary purpose of ARGUS is to provide a robust, real-time facial recognition and analysis platform. It bridges the gap between mobile surveillance (via mobile device cameras) and centralized monitoring (via a laptop or dashboard). ARGUS is designed to identify multiple individuals seamlessly, simulate facial aging for predictive matching, and maintain a secure, actionable activity log.

## Aim
ARGUS aims to deliver:
1. **Real-time Face Recognition:** Low-latency streaming of video from a portable device (like a smartphone) to a central monitor, processing faces against a known database.
2. **Predictive Analysis (AI Aging):** The ability to simulate the aging process of a subject to compare current images with older database records.
3. **Comprehensive Monitoring:** Centralized alerting and logging to ensure operators are immediately aware of matches, unknown subjects, and system events.

## End User
The end users of ARGUS are primarily security personnel, system operators, and investigators who need to monitor live feeds or analyze images against a database of known individuals. They interact with the system via a web-based dashboard on their laptops or desktop computers.

## Target Audience
The target audience or core customer base for ARGUS includes:
- **Law Enforcement & Investigators:** For matching suspects against databases, especially using the AI aging feature to track individuals over time.
- **Security Teams (Corporate & Private):** For real-time premises monitoring, identifying authorized personnel vs. unknown individuals using continuity cameras.
- **Event Organizers:** For managing access control and monitoring crowd safety.

## Impact
By providing an accessible, web-based platform that utilizes powerful WebSockets and AI models (like Face-API), ARGUS significantly lowers the barrier to entry for advanced surveillance and biometric analysis. 
- It reduces the need for expensive, proprietary hardware by allowing standard smartphones to act as secure streaming devices.
- The AI aging integration ensures high accuracy in long-term missing persons cases or long-term investigations.
- The activity logging and alerting mechanics ensure a higher standard of accountability and rapid response to security events.
